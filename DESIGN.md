## Overview

Tsukiyomi adopts the **Figma Color-Block Editorial System**:
- **No gradients, no glass, no pills**: Every surface is flat and opaque with crisp 1px–1.5px solid borders and structured rectangular corners (`2px`, `6px`, `8px`, `12px`).
- **Oversized Pastel Color-Block Sections**: Full-width story panels in **Lilac (`#C5B0F4`)**, **Lime (`#DCEEB1`)**, **Cream (`#F4ECD6`)**, **Mint (`#C8E6CD`)**, **Coral (`#F3C9B6`)**, and **Deep Navy (`#1F1D3D`)** organize the Home feed into distinct editorial chapters.
- **Monospace Eyebrows & Tight Headlines**: Uppercase monospace category markers (`01 // CONTINUE READING`, `02 // EXTERNAL DISCOVERY`) paired with bold, negative-tracked headlines.
